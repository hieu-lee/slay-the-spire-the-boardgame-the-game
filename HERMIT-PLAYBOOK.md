# Hermit Playbook

Live strategy and attempt log for **solo Hermit**, starting at A0 and advancing only after a recorded Act III win. The source of truth is the board-game implementation in `src/game/downfall/hermit.ts`, `src/game/combat/`, `src/game/enemies.ts`, and `src/game/map.ts`, not PC Downfall card numbers. Play and inspect the live game **only through WebMCP**. After each finished run, download the log, explicitly record the campaign result, verify the solo leaderboard, and update this file **before** another run.

## Mechanics to exploit

- **Early three-Louse check:** A floor-3 encounter can spawn three Lice with a combined 4–6 incoming damage while their first hits each trigger 2 Block. The starter-heavy `Snapshot`/`Feint`/`Low Profile` deck cannot necessarily kill one before the damage arrives: do not spend 1 HP at Ancient Temple on a relic such as Bird-Faced Urn when the deck has no Powers and the next fight precedes the campfire. Prefer a no-damage event branch or an earlier rest, and reserve Snapshot for a Chamber play that grants its Dead On Block.
- **Removing Parasite costs 2 maximum HP** (`src/game/acquisition.ts`), even when an event calls the removal free; Bonfire Spirits also charges 1 current HP for offering a Curse. At 6–8 max HP, weigh both penalties against thinning one dead draw. Weak is spent on each attack you make; Vulnerable affects every hit in the *next attack action* before one token is spent. Vulnerable doubles damage unless the attacker has Weak, in which case the two cancel; account for multi-hit actions and enemy attack order.

- Hermit starts at **8 HP, 3 Energy, two Chamber slots**, with Covet, Snapshot, five Strikes, and four Defends. Solo Neow grants Loaded Die and 2 gold after rewards. Every combat starts with draw 1 and a mandatory Load. **Playing from the Chamber, not merely owning a card with Dead On, activates Dead On**; copies inherit it. Loaded cards leave the draw cycle until played or replaced. Replacing a slot discards its occupant.
- Always consider the mandatory initial Load: load Snapshot if its damage plus equal Block will matter; load a Strike/Defend to protect a stronger next-turn draw when Snapshot is needed immediately. Covet retains and loads one card for free; save it until a card worth staging appears or an urgent dead-on play requires setup. Avoid filling both slots with junk when expecting a high-value reward card.
- Rapid Fire repeats **the whole card effect** (including block, debuffs, Dead On when played from Chamber); extra Rapid Fires do not recursively copy copies. Block and Energy cap; avoid wasting duplicated Block or overfilling Energy. `Headshot` only damages with Dead On. Snapshot's Dead On base Block matches its **modified hit damage** after Strength, Weak, Vulnerable, and per-hit caps, even versus a blocked/near-dead target; Block bonuses apply afterward, while Buffer prevents the bonus entirely.
- Count `Snapshot`'s modified hit damage as base Block when Dead On triggers; Strength can make a 2-damage Snapshot grant 5 Block before Block bonuses. Verify the actual Block after resolving it, especially before auto-ending a Time Warp turn. `Maintenance+` makes starter Strikes free and adds 1 damage; this is an excellent early power with nine starters. `Overwhelming Power` draws 2 once per turn after two attacks and becomes easier to trigger with those free Strikes.
- A Chamber is not a free play: ordinarily pay the card's cost unless a free-play effect (`Fan the Hammer`, `Eye Of The Storm`, `Called Shot`, `Eternal Form`) says otherwise. Start-of-turn abilities can force or sequence Chamber choices **before** the normal draw; inspect all options and the pending draw before choosing order/targets. Curses can be beneficial on Load (Scorn: block, Horror: Weak/Vulnerable, Malice: row damage, Undead: temporary Strength, Grudge: targeted damage) but are unplayable and clog draw/Chamber; choose them only with repeatable loading/discard and a payoff.
- `Battle Buddies` gives 1 Energy each turn but forces two opening discards: reserve defense and a payoff, discard redundant starters. `Incense Burner` can cap *total* HP loss for the round at 1 on die 6; Loaded Die can trigger that ability instead of taking its own +1 Energy. `Vantage` makes the next Attack Rapid Fire even when played from the Chamber; against Flying Byrds each hit still deals at most 1, so repeat hits or row attacks rather than relying on a large single hit.
- **Time Eater:** A0 has 60 HP and revives once at 30 HP with +1 Strength. Its three-turn loop is 2×2 Attack, three Slimed with no attack, then 6 Attack and +1 Strength; the UI intent includes Strength and Weak, so do not subtract Weak again. Time Warp limits plays to **5 / 4 / 3**, and each Rapid Fire copy counts as another play, potentially ending the turn automatically. Budget Block **before** committing the final card/copy, then plan for the next rotation and the second HP bar. After the standard draw and roll, `Smoking Barrel` can discard a staged card for three draws during ordered start-of-turn ability resolution; keep staged `Snapshot` when its Dead On Block is needed, or discard a spare card when the extra draw is worth more. `Magnum` fixes its Rapid Fire count from the cards in hand before discarding them; with 3 Strength, its original hit plus four 5-damage copies killed the 25-HP second bar at the five-play limit in campaign 42. Eternal Form's mandatory start-of-turn Load can force replacement of a full Chamber; don't stage two indispensable cards when one must be discarded next turn. At 6 maximum HP from Mark of Pain, starter Defends and Snapshot alone are not a sufficient defensive engine for this boss; prioritize Weak, repeatable multi-Block, and a real defensive potion over more attack copies or draw.

## Build and draft

- **Slime Boss's second split turn can be deadlier than the first.** A 10-HP Large Slime's die rotation can hit for 4, doubled to 8 by player Vulnerable. Four Defends can block the first split turn but leave no damage or staged defense for the next draw. Gambler's Brew can change the shared die before resolution: face 1 reduced an 8-damage intent to 2 in campaign 13, but status-heavy draws still made the following turn lethal. Draft repeatable Block/Weak or keep a staged payoff through the split; do not prefer a conditional Headshot to a reliable defensive card solely for boss burst.

- **Early reliable picks:** `Pistol Whip` (2 damage for 1; from Chamber refunds 1 Energy and Loads), `Quickdraw`/`Feint` (draw and Load), `Body Armor` (2 Block, 0 cost if played from Chamber), `Dive` (2 Block for 1), `Itchy Trigger` (two 1-damage hits for 1), `High-Caliber` (two hits **and** two Block for 2), `Fan the Hammer` (2 damage and a free Chamber play), `Low Profile` (0-cost Block/Load). Prefer immediate damage or defense over slow Powers until survival is stable. `Misfire` is a 0-cost row hit, strong into multi-enemy rows. Skip a reward if it dilutes the deck without solving a specific fight.
- **Strong payoffs:** `Showdown` adds 1 to **each** Rapid Fire hit; `No Holds Barred` adds another copy to Rapid Fire cards; `High Noon` makes every starter Strike a Rapid Fire attack; `Magnum` copies per other card in hand, but discards that hand; `Roundhouse Kick` supplies 3 row damage and Vulnerable; `Coalescence` draws 2 and loads up to 2 for zero; `Eye Of The Storm` plays all staged cards free. `Flash Powder` gives two Weak applications and Block on its two plays; prioritize it when the upcoming enemy hits repeatedly. `Fully Loaded` adds slots but is only worth a turn's tempo when the deck has enough valuable cards to stage.
- **Dead On loop:** stage `Pistol Whip`, `Wide Open` (Vulnerable), `Deadeye` (Strength), `Trick Shot` (draw proportional to damage dealt), `Snapshot` (Block), or `Headshot` (5/7 damage only when staged). `Tracking Shots` can reload itself from hand; use it to reserve a 3/4-damage shot for a later turn. Dead On combos depend on a way to play the staged card, enough energy, and a target that survives setup.
- **Split-boss survival:** Slime Boss has 22 HP at A0 and rotates three Slimed → attack 3 plus two Slimed → attack 6. On death it queues **Large Slime (10 HP), Acid Slime (5 HP), and Spike Slime (5 HP)** for the *next* turn; their simultaneous attacks can total **8 damage** immediately. Treat killing the boss as the start of a fresh, potentially lethal three-enemy turn, not the end of the fight. Check the remaining draw pile and chamber *before* delivering the last hit. On a harmless Slimed turn, delaying the kill to stage defense or preserve a draw potion can be safer than spending the last potion to kill immediately. Against an 8-damage split turn at 5 HP, one Defend and three free 3-damage Strikes cannot prevent lethal damage: killing either 5-HP slime takes two Strikes and leaves 6 incoming, only one of which is blocked.
- **Split-turn Energy budget:** `Flash Powder` from a `Called Shot`-discounted Chamber gives two Block and two targeted Weak, but that alone does **not** cover Large Slime's 3-damage weakened hit plus a weakened Spike Slime's 1-damage hit at 1 HP. In attempt 3, an **Energy Potion bought for 2 gold instead of the unused 3-gold `Eye Of The Storm`** would have paid for both drawn Defends after killing Acid Slime: four total Block against four incoming damage. Reserve a Potion/extra Energy or bring a real multi-enemy block card *before* the boss split. If `Overwhelming Power` will draw after the second attack, budget Energy for the resulting Defends rather than assuming the draw itself solves lethal damage.
- **Check the whole status-polluted draw cycle before splitting:** In attempt 4, the boss died cleanly on turn 3 at 8 HP, but the split turn drew four Defends and four Slimed (including a three-card Dead Branch draw). The draw pile contained only two more Slimed and one Strike; Whale Ale drew only those two remaining cards and **did not reshuffle mid-effect**, wasting half its draw. Large Slime's two Weak stuck across turns until spent by attacks, while new Slimed/Daze kept starving the deck. Upgrade/add real multi-target mitigation or an attack that kills a 5-HP slime in one play; do not rely on draw potions when the remaining draw pile is all statuses. If the split-turn draw is forecast to be all statuses, consider taking the boss's 6-damage turn with enough staged Block rather than initiating an unwinnable split immediately.
- **Status and relic caution:** `Slimed` costs 1 Energy to Exhaust and does **not** trigger Charon's Ashes by itself; that relic only offers its 2-damage choice on a matching start-of-turn die. Gremlin Horn's draw/Energy depends on die faces 4/5 and may never trigger in a short boss fight. Do not spend all gold on speculative die relics when guaranteed Block or a draw potion solves the known split turn. A Weak token is spent on the next attack, so use a cheap Strike to clear it before a high-damage staged Deadeye when energy and defense permit.
- **Act III Donu/Deca needs sustained mitigation:** Deca attacks **3×3** on its first turn, then adds Daze/Slimed; Donu gives **both enemies +1 Strength** first, then attacks **4×3** on turn two. At 8 current HP, the turn-two 12-damage attack requires at least **5 Block** to survive at 1 HP; more is needed if already hurt. A quick Portal to the last merchant/fire forfeits several chances to draft Weak, repeatable Block, and damage scaling; do not take it with only starter Defends and 8 HP. Prefer a defensive potion/Weak engine over a 4-damage Mystery Potion, and compare *both* boss turns against the reachable hand before committing the route. Upgraded Roundhouse Kick hits both bosses via its row-and-boss rule; a Liquid Void replay deals 5 then 10 to each but does not make up for missing defense.
- **Act II Bronze Automaton needs defense on consecutive attacks:** its 55 HP and 19-HP Orb outlast simple starter damage. The boss buffs Strength on turns 1/3/5, attacks twice on turns 2/6 (4 total and then 8 after Strength), and makes a heavy single hit on turn 4 (9 after Strength); the Orb can also attack for 3 on the early turns. Kill the Orb before the heavy hit, but **reserve at least 6 Block on turn 6 if at 3 HP**. With both Chamber slots occupied, play the staged defensive card *before* Feint/Load if you need its Block this turn: otherwise the forced replacement can discard a needed defense. `Smoking Barrel+` is powerful draw but no substitute for actual Block; an extra defensive potion or repeatable Weak is more valuable here than another free-Strike synergy relic.
- **Curse route (conditional):** `Desperado` gains Rapid Fire with a curse staged; `Short Fuse` costs less with starters staged/in hand and hits an entire row twice; `Specter` repeats Block plus draw for each curse in hand/Chamber. `Purgatory` becomes affordable with curses, `Cursed Weapon` scales with them. Do not take an unplayable curse simply for scaling unless the deck has repeatable Load, the HP cushion, and a way to spend its chamber slot.
- **Upgrades:** prioritize a payoff actually being played. `Pistol Whip+` becomes 3 damage per play; `Headshot+` is 7 Dead On damage; `Body Armor+` is 3 Block and still free from Chamber; `Feint+` draws 3; `Snapshot+` starts at 3 damage before modifiers and grants Block equal to its modified hit (zero if Buffer prevents it); `Itchy Trigger+` gives three hits; starter Strike+ doubles damage if High Noon/Maintenance makes them efficient. Rest instead when 8-max-HP survival is at risk and the next route contains mandatory fights.

## Before every room and fight

1. **Stop before choosing a room.** Inspect reachable map exits, future branches, boss, HP, potions, gold, deck, and current relics. Plan through the next campfire/merchant/boss rather than optimizing only the immediate room. At low HP prefer safe events/fire/shops with affordable healing over unknown dark tokens or elites; seek combats for card quality only when the next two fights are survivable. A boss victory heals for the next act, but the current boss still requires surviving every turn.
   - **Event survival is not guaranteed by Fairy in a Bottle.** The first recorded run died instantly at **2/8 HP** when Wheel of Change rolled 6 and applied its 2-HP loss, despite still carrying Fairy. At ≤2 HP, account for an event's possible direct HP loss *before* committing to an event-heavy route; prioritize healing at an available campfire, even if the alternate route has later danger. At an already revealed Wheel, spinning is compulsory; the crucial decision was the prior route.
2. **Before engaging a known enemy**, read its entry in `src/game/enemies.ts` and any summons/ascension variant in `src/game/run/encounters.ts`; write down turn-one attack, rotation/cube or die possibilities, summons, and priority kill. Plan a kill/block line using the current deck and potions. Avoid entering at 1–2 HP merely because the enemy sometimes does not attack.
3. **At turn start**, inspect start-turn abilities, relics, intent/targets, hand, draw/discard piles, Chamber, retained cards, Energy, potions, and statuses. Sequence active abilities so draw/Load improves the hand rather than overwrites a critical staged card. Estimate the next draw from remaining pile and likely reshuffle; keep Covet/retained defense if next turn's known attack is worse. Check whether Weak, a kill, free Chamber play, or potion prevents more HP loss than a raw Defend.
4. **Before ending turn**, compute each remaining hit after Weak/strength and target-specific Block, account for Vulnerable, and compare to current HP. Spend a potion proactively to prevent otherwise lethal or major unblocked damage; do not hoard three potions into death or spend a scarce potion on a harmless turn. Prioritize deleting a dangerous minion entirely over partial damage that leaves its intent active. Save retained cards when current defenses suffice and their future value exceeds the immediate play.
5. **After the fight**, check damage taken, card choice/skip, actual next map options, and adjust the planned route. When the run ends, download its log via the visible control and store it locally if the tool provides a path. Press **Record campaign result** after defeat or **Stop and record result** after victory, confirm the new solo leaderboard entry, then record the failure mode and one actionable correction below. If A0 is won, select A1 next; repeat through the highest unlocked ascension, at most A13.
