## Starting rules and decisions

- Silent has 9 maximum HP at A0/A1 and 8 from A2. The starter deck is five Strikes, five Defends, `Neutralize` (free 1 damage and Weak), and `Survivor` (2 Block, discard one); Ring of the Snake draws two extra cards at combat start. Historical entries retain the rules observed at their recording time; use these current rules and live source/intents for new runs.
- Before ending a turn, check each enemy's current intent, HP, Block, Poison, row, and attack order against own HP, Block, Weak/Vulnerable, available Energy, hand, and remaining draw. Poison ticks before enemy attacks, so a lethal tick cancels that enemy's attack. Count all targets of row attacks and avoid spending a defensive potion on a turn that is already covered.
- Consult in-run `get_stats` with the current deck before drafting, buying, or upgrading cards. Use `ascension: "all"` for the full archive, an integer for exact Ascension, or `"0+"` through `"10+"` for a threshold; no separate minimum filter is needed. Before this marathon there were **25** Standard Silent runs across ascensions, but only **3** at A1; after five runs there are **30** overall and **12** at A2. Treat these small, selected samples as directional rather than causal evidence. Favor immediate damage and reliable mitigation when weak early turns threaten the run.
- Inspect the entire reachable map before routing. At a merchant, count gold and compare removal, potions, relics, and cards against the next mandatory encounters; at events, compare the worst HP outcome with the next rest and boss. Record outcomes explicitly rather than merely leaving the end screen.
- Against Time Eater, read the current turn's changing card cap before playing; Shivs are not cards and remain usable after the cap. Its Haste restores 30 HP below A10 or 32 at A10+, removes Weak/Vulnerable, adds Strength, and **keeps Poison**. `Storm of Steel` holds at most five Shivs; each excess Shiv gets an immediate overflow target. With `Accuracy` and Red Skull Strength, each Shiv deals 3, so check overflow and Poison lethal before spending scarce defensive Energy.
- Three Act II Cultists each gain Strength every turn: by turn three they can collectively attack for 8 or more. Poison applied to only one enemy per turn cannot stabilize the board on its own. Prioritize early row damage, killing one before its attack, and enough repeatable Block/Weak; do not let an ethereal `Apparition` expire without checking whether its HP cap is worth using. `Piercing Wail` applies Weak across their shared row, but one Wail and two starter Defends supply only 3 Block against their combined 6 damage at turn four. A single-target Weak Potion cannot cover the whole wave.
- Slime Boss has 22 HP below A10 or 23 at A10+ and **only splits on defeat**, into Large Slime (10 HP), Acid Slime (5 HP), and Spike Slime (5 HP) on the next turn; A10+ gives Large Slime one Strength. Killing the boss is not the end of the encounter: prepare for an immediate combined wave, then a heavy attack turn. Spike Slime applies Vulnerable even through fully blocked damage. A low-damage deck of starter Strikes plus `Blade Dance+` and no Poison let the boss inflict six HP loss and could not prevent the next wave's lethal 8+4+2 attack. Route and draft for both the boss and its adds; do not treat a boss at 1 HP as nearly won.
- Weak reduces **every hit in one attack action** by one, then consumes one token after that action, even if blocked. Extra tokens protect later attack actions, not extra hits in the same volley. Vulnerable likewise applies to the whole attack action before one token is spent; Weak and Vulnerable cancel when both apply. Do not subtract Weak or multiply Vulnerable again when the live intent already includes them. Current Donu/Deca each have 50 HP below A10 or 55 at A10+, and attack **twice**, not three times. Deca opens with 3×2 while Donu buffs Strength; Donu then attacks for 4×2 while Deca adds statuses. One Weak reduces that Donu volley to 3×2. Older three-hit ledger entries describe a since-fixed implementation, not the current boss. Hexaghost has **no Buffer**; older Buffer references are also historical. Setting up Poison is not itself mitigation. Preserve a defensive potion if possible, but Transient may force earlier use. Prefer reliable mitigation and draw over another slow setup card.
- Awakened One's Reborn form starts at 50 HP and clears the first form's Poison and attached Corpse Explosion; do not count on either carrying across. Two Cultists attack alongside the first form, so clear them while developing `Noxious Fumes+` and `Footwork+`. Reapply Poison after the phase change; `Choke` adds damage per existing Poison and Weak without consuming them. Incense Burner triggers automatically on a six: copying it with Loaded Die is redundant, so choose Ink Bottle's draw or Energy instead. `Bullet Time+` makes the current hand free but prevents further draws, so play draw Skills first; free Defends cover an attack only when Poison will not kill first.

Lessons from the completed marathon:

- Sozu prevents gaining new Potions, not using an existing Potion: run ten successfully used a previously held Vulnerable Potion after acquiring Sozu. Vulnerable is consumed by one Attack action; separate manual Shivs do not all receive its bonus. Prefer a multi-hit card when maximizing a single Vulnerable token.
- Charon's Ashes must be considered before resolving start of turn. Exhaust an unwanted hand card for two damage, but remember enemy Block can stop that damage. Against Spiker it does not trigger Thorns. Loaded Die can copy Captain's Wheel for three Block instead of one Energy when safe Power setup matters more than another attack.

- Book of Stabbing's Strength affects every multi-hit stab; Weak subtracts only one per hit, not its full stack. Enter forced Act II elites with repeatable mitigation or a defensive potion. Losing Ring of the Snake to N'loth can make setup appreciably worse. Black Blood heals two only after a surviving combat, not when first acquired.

- Wrist Blade Shivs count as Attacks for Finisher; Envenom applies Poison on every Finisher hit. Reserve the Energy for Finisher after building its hit count. Footwork increases each Block icon separately: base Deflect gains four Block with a Shiv, two without. Preserve a Shiv until conditional Block or Slice resolves. Frozen Core adds one Block at end of turn. Awakened One Reborn has zero initial Strength below A10; first-form Poison and Weak reset, but played Powers persist.

- Dagger Spray+ deals **one damage three times**, not two damage twice. A saved Cloud alone cannot cover a seven-damage split: plan at least two additional Block or immediate kills. Against Slime Boss, clear Slimed on its idle turn when excess Energy would otherwise be wasted, and retain defense/draw rather than allowing a contaminated shuffle to dictate the wave hand. Even a no-damage regular-act route needs reliable boss mitigation, not just Poison.

- Collector's Strength buff raises subsequent Torch damage; use the live intents and observed resolution order rather than assuming its buff precedes every Torch's current attack. Persistent boss Poison is not sufficient if the second Torch wave remains alive. Accelerate boss damage or reserve row damage and potions for that wave; Burn-heavy hands need discard available, not merely Energy banking.
- Four Energy, two upgraded Backflips, four Strike removals, and repeatable Fumes/Footwork supported a successful lean deck. Footwork+ still costs two Energy; its upgrade grants Retain, not a cost reduction. Malaise+ adds one Weak/Poison, while Catalyst+ triples Poison. Against Awakened One, reserve Catalyst for Reborn when the first form is already safely dying to persistent Poison; its new summon clears Poison and Weak. At A4 its Reborn form starts with zero Strength, unlike the A10 Power-based Strength penalty.

- Against Gremlin Wizards, take an available exact early kill before slow Power setup. Route toward a merchant and defensive potions; retain cycling to escape Slimed-heavy hands. Bomb exhausting in Slime Boss's first form leaves no repeatable damage for its summons.

- Buy repeatable mitigation, Energy, or accelerated setup before another situational attack. Ten starter cards in a 26-card deck made a full-health boss entrance insufficient. Against Awakened One, every active Power adds one to each first-form hit; neither upgraded Wraith Form nor Envenom is safe setup when the resulting HP loss is still lethal.
- Champ's Fury clears Weak/Vulnerable but **keeps Poison**. Awakened One instead returns as a newly summoned enemy with **zero Poison**. Its A3 Reborn form has no Strength-per-Power bonus; that bonus begins at A10.
- Darkling Regrow restores dead allies at four HP and clears their tokens/Strength. Bank Shivs for Red Skull when a shuffle is imminent, distribute Poison for simultaneous end-turn kills, and avoid killing just one repeatedly.
- Loaded Die can copy Oddly Smooth Stone for two Block instead of one Energy. Abacus must be considered before resolving start-turn effects; spend it where advancing the die can improve mitigation, not merely because it is available.
- Footwork boosts each separate Block effect: Deflect with a Shiv gives four Block, Deflect+ gives five, discarded-this-turn Blur+ gives six, and Dodge and Roll gives four. Keep one Shiv for these conditional bonuses when lethal does not require spending it.
- Wrist Blade boosts printed zero-cost attacks, including Neutralize, Slice, Dramatic Entrance, and Shivs; Madness/Bullet Time do not qualify a printed one-cost attack. Akabeko boosts every Flechettes hit. Burst plus Blade Dance+ produces six usable Shivs when the overflow attack is targeted instead of skipped.
- Reborn's Void automatically spends available Energy to exhaust each drawn Slimed. Account for that drain before using draw cards or relying on Bullet Time. Avoid drawing when existing Shivs plus Poison already give exact lethal.
- Sozu's Energy is not automatically worth losing every future potion: this run repeatedly declined Fairy and could not bridge an unlucky boss hand. Prefer reliable setup and fewer starters over additional marginal cards. Deadly Poison+ reduces cost to zero; it does not add a second Poison. Loaded Die copying Sundial grants two Energy instead of the default one.
