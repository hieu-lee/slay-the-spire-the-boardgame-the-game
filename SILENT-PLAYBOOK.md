# Silent solo playbook

Live strategy and campaign ledger for five Standard solo Silent runs, starting at Ascension 1. Record each result, download and review its log, verify the solo leaderboard, and update this file before the next run.

## Starting rules and decisions

- Silent has 9 maximum HP. The starter deck is five Strikes, five Defends, `Neutralize` (free 1 damage and Weak), and `Survivor` (2 Block, discard one); Ring of the Snake draws two extra cards at combat start.
- Before ending a turn, check each enemy's current intent, HP, Block, Poison, row, and attack order against own HP, Block, Weak/Vulnerable, available Energy, hand, and remaining draw. Poison ticks before enemy attacks, so a lethal tick cancels that enemy's attack. Count all targets of row attacks and avoid spending a defensive potion on a turn that is already covered.
- Consult in-run `get_stats` with the current deck before drafting, buying, or upgrading cards. Use `ascension: "all"` for the full archive, an integer for exact Ascension, or `"0+"` through `"10+"` for a threshold; no separate minimum filter is needed. Before this marathon there were **25** Standard Silent runs across ascensions, but only **3** at A1; after five runs there are **30** overall and **12** at A2. Treat these small, selected samples as directional rather than causal evidence. Favor immediate damage and reliable mitigation when weak early turns threaten the run.
- Inspect the entire reachable map before routing. At a merchant, count gold and compare removal, potions, relics, and cards against the next mandatory encounters; at events, compare the worst HP outcome with the next rest and boss. Record outcomes explicitly rather than merely leaving the end screen.
- Against Time Eater, read the current turn's changing card cap before playing; use all Shivs before the final allowed card, since the cap also locks Shiv actions. Its Haste restores 30 HP, removes Weak/Vulnerable, adds Strength, and **keeps Poison**. `Storm of Steel` holds at most five Shivs; each excess Shiv gets an immediate overflow target. With `Accuracy` and Red Skull Strength, each Shiv deals 3, so check overflow and Poison lethal before spending scarce defensive Energy.
- Three Act II Cultists each gain Strength every turn: by turn three they can collectively attack for 8 or more. Poison applied to only one enemy per turn cannot stabilize the board on its own. Prioritize early row damage, killing one before its attack, and enough repeatable Block/Weak; do not let an ethereal `Apparition` expire without checking whether its HP cap is worth using. `Piercing Wail` applies Weak across their shared row, but one Wail and two starter Defends supply only 3 Block against their combined 6 damage at turn four. A single-target Weak Potion cannot cover the whole wave.
- Slime Boss has 22 HP and **only splits on defeat**, into Large Slime (10 HP), Acid Slime (5 HP), and Spike Slime (5 HP) on the next turn. Killing the boss is not the end of the encounter: prepare for an immediate combined wave, then a heavy attack turn. Spike Slime applies Vulnerable even through fully blocked damage. A low-damage deck of starter Strikes plus `Blade Dance+` and no Poison let the boss inflict six HP loss and could not prevent the next wave's lethal 8+4+2 attack. Route and draft for both the boss and its adds; do not treat a boss at 1 HP as nearly won.
- Donu+Deca each begin at 50 HP: Deca opens with a 3×3 attack while Donu buffs Strength, then Donu attacks for 4×3 with the buff while Deca adds Daze/Slimed. Without Vulnerable, one Weak lowers all three hits to 3×3, still lethal at full 8 HP without at least 2 Block or equivalent prevention; killing Donu first avoids the volley. Setting up Poison is not itself mitigation. Preserve a defensive potion for this boss if possible, but Transient's escalating attacks may force earlier use. Prefer reliable multi-hit mitigation and a lean enough deck to draw it over another slow setup card.
- Awakened One's Reborn form starts at 50 HP and clears the first form's Poison and attached Corpse Explosion; do not count on either carrying across. Two Cultists attack alongside the first form, so clear them while developing `Noxious Fumes+` and `Footwork+`. Reapply Poison after the phase change; `Choke` adds damage per existing Poison and Weak without consuming them. Incense Burner triggers automatically on a six: copying it with Loaded Die is redundant, so choose Ink Bottle's draw or Energy instead. `Bullet Time+` makes the current hand free but prevents further draws, so play draw Skills first; free Defends cover an attack only when Poison will not kill first.

Total includes the repeatedly cached conversation prefix, so uncached input and generated output are the useful marginal signals; phase transitions make these approximate, not a billing ledger. Combat dominates through separate card/Shiv selection and enemy-target calls. Once deployed, `interact_with_game` can take `targetLabel` with a listed card/Shiv `controlId` to select a **unique visible, currently unavailable enemy** and target it in one call; a failed or non-targeting first choice still returns its settled state. Keep using `since` for lossless deltas and `inspect_game` after uncertain results. Do not chain choices that need inspection between steps.
