Play on iPhone

Install the landscape-only native app for solo and online multiplayer. It's free, but each iPhone needs its own one-time SideStore setup.

  1. On your own computer and iPhone, follow SideStore's prerequisites and installation guide. Use your own Apple Account; don't share passwords with the game publisher.
  2. Connect to Wi-Fi, turn on LocalDevVPN, and complete SideStore's initial refresh.
  3. Return to this page on your iPhone and tap the button below.

Try installing in SideStore

This roughly 1 GB IPA has not yet been installed through SideStore on a physical iPhone. Large-app signing can fail on some devices. Treat this as a preview until a real-device install and refresh are confirmed.

Apple's free app signature lasts seven days. SideStore tries to refresh it automatically while Wi-Fi and LocalDevVPN are available; if that fails, the app can expire and need attention. This isn't an App Store installation.
