# Guardian Playbook

Strategy and attempt log for 12 solo Guardian Standard runs, stopped after run 12 at the user's request. The campaign started at A0 and increased Ascension only after a recorded Act III victory. Choose **Slay the Spire**, not the Downfall campaign, when starting; Guardian's character-specific card pool and ruleset still apply. Use only the game's WebMCP `inspect_game` and `interact_with_game` to play. After **every** finished run, download and review the run log, explicitly select `Record campaign result` after defeat or `Stop and record result` after victory, verify the solo Guardian leaderboard entry, and update this file before the next run.

## Verified source mechanics

- Guardian starts at **9 HP, 3 Energy, Attack Mode**, with four Strikes (1 damage), four Defends (1 Block), `Curl Up` (2 Energy, 2 Block; gain a Vigor in Defense Mode), and `Twin Slam` (2 Energy, 2 damage plus 1 in Attack Mode). The starting relic enters Attack Mode at each combat start. A2 removes one maximum/current HP.
- Guardian can choose a Mode Shift at the start of a turn and on certain cards. The start-of-turn choice must be resolved **before** planning around the final hand and intents. Attack Mode adds spent Vigor to **each hit** on qualifying Attack/Skill cards; Defense Mode adds spent Vigor to **each Block icon**. Both available and spent Vigor share a four-token supply, so freshly gained Vigor can be capped until spent tokens return. Spend Vigor explicitly on a card only after checking its cost, target, and whether the next hit or Block matters more.
- Socket cards receive a Gem when acquired; reward drafts reveal two distinct Gems, and the chosen Gem's effect is attached to that card. A Socket card's printed Block or damage and attached Gem resolve together. Choose Gem combinations for the actual deck and combat rather than treating every Socket as free value. `Aquamarine` ends further card plays, so attach/use with care.
- The card pool includes cheap draw/tempo (`Crystal Edge`, `Hack`, `Fortify`, `Incinerate`, `Preprogram`), reliable Block (`Spheric Shield`, `Disrupt`, `Harden`, `Evade`), and damage (`Fierce Bash`, `Walker Claw`, `Poly Beam`, `Giga Beam`); specific picks depend on Energy, Gems, and the current defensive budget. `Overload` draws four and adds Dazed but does not shift mode; `Giga Beam` deals damage and locks Attack Mode for the rest of combat **only when played in Attack Mode**. `Body Crash` spends existing Block for **two** X-damage hits, not one X-squared hit. `Time Sifter` recovers spent tokens, not a fifth token. Do not assume printed multi-hit or multi-Block icons resolve as a single effect.
- A spent Vigor remains active for the whole turn across Mode Shifts: it boosts Attack hits in Attack Mode and Block icons in Defense Mode. Attack-mode `Guardian Whirl` at X=0 still hits for one with one active Vigor. `Twin Slam+` has two distinct hits, each receiving the Vigor bonus; `Body Crash+` can convert existing Block into two free hits. `Exploit Gems+` socketed with Tourmaline generates Energy and immediately spends a Vigor, so sequence it before multi-hit attacks when possible.
- `Gem Finder` can draw a revealed *socketed card* during Scry, not just loose Gems; a socketed `Harden` entered the hand immediately in run four. `Dented Plate` provides its extra Energy only while Guardian has at least 5 HP; at A1, advancing from Act I healed Guardian from 4/9 to full before the Act II opener.
- `Spiker Protocol+` deals 4 to each enemy intending to attack at end of turn, **once per enemy**, even when the enemy intends two hits. Run seven's live build incorrectly doubled it against Bronze Automaton; the combat resolver and focused regression check were corrected afterward. `Self-Forming Clay` must be manually activated after HP loss and is once per combat; neither Defense Mode nor keeping that mode grants Block by itself.

## Route and turn procedure

1. At every map choice inspect the **reachable** rooms and upcoming boss. Prioritize campfires for healing when low, and avoid optional elites until the deck can block their opening damage. Draft offense before chaining events into a forced multi-enemy fight; an early portal removes chances to draft defense for an Act III boss.
2. Resolve every mandatory start-of-turn choice, then inspect HP, mode, available/spent Vigor, Energy, Block, hand/draw/discard, Gems, potions, enemy HP and intent. Do not reuse stale control IDs or blindly replay a timed-out action; inspect again.
3. Compute immediate lethal first, including separate Vigor bonuses for each attack hit and each target. If no lethal, calculate the Block needed for **every** attack and status/Burn, accounting for Weak, Vulnerable, and on-death reactions before spending Energy or Vigor.
4. Compare optional Mode Shift against this turn's Block and damage; save enough Energy for a defend-or-kill line. Order draw/setup, Weak/Vulnerable, Vigor spending and multi-hit payoff; re-inspect after draws, random effects, enemy deaths, and new targets.
5. End only after checking free plays, playable Gems, the next turn's mode, potion availability, and whether an apparent boss kill spawns a second phase. Retain a useful potion for the dangerous boss turn, but spend it before an otherwise fatal hit. At the Collector, remove Torch Heads quickly and preserve enough Block or a potion for the turn-three 6+2+2 attack: vulnerable boss damage alone does not prevent their attacks. `Repulsor` and Garnet both generate Dazed, so account for hand dilution before taking both into a long boss fight.
